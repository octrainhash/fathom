# Fathom 1.2

A free Windows app for everything you do with files. It runs on your own PC: nothing is uploaded and there is no account.

## New in 1.2
- **Subtitles for any movie:** make them by listening (every spoken language, word timing), translate them into 140+ languages, fix, shift, merge and burn them in.
- **Toolbox:** barcodes and QR codes, searchable PDFs (OCR), background remover, watermark remover, colour palettes, contact sheets, waveform pictures, and text and developer helpers.
- **Workflows and watch folders:** chain steps and run them on a folder automatically.
- **About 70 more file types** for convert and search.
- **Right-click > Send to > Fathom,** portable mode, and settings backup and restore.
- **One-folder installer:** pick one folder and everything (the app, its data, every program and every model) goes inside it. Nothing downloads later when you use a feature.
- **Problems and Fixes** in the setup window: pick a problem, or describe it, and get a fix file.

## Download
**fathom.zip** is the only download. Extract it, double-click **Fathom-Setup.vbs**, choose Everything or Lite, pick a folder and press Install. Everything needs about 30 GB free and a long first download; Lite needs about 3 GB.

Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit.
